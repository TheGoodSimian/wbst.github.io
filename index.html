What is this place, even

Hello?

This is where I, yours truly, upload miscellaneous things of various kinds. It's a place where I try to maintain a healthier, freerier presence online, away from the exploitative and psychologically draining net of platforms known as 'social media' (a misnomer if ever there was one).

It's also my humble contribution to the IndieWeb movement which gave me the final push I needed to move away from even passive presence on those networks. If it inspires even a few people to do the same, It'll make me very happy. Having your own page where you are in control over how you interface with others online-the medium, the frame, and the content through which others see you and your work-is both comforting and liberating. On a larger scale, it has implications for how communities, nay, society at large, develops online, free from the undertones of mass manipluation, uniformity and commerce. Of course, I don't pretend to be some beacon of freedom in the bleak landscape of modern internet. I just personally don't like the way it has made me and others I know feel and act. I overwhelmingly don't trust the people behind those platforms. They don't, for the most part, seem like people I'd like to shake hands or do business with, let alone accept 'free' services from. I think that the alternative, which is growing together a diverse, decentralised, nonconformist net, would be pretty, pretty cool.

Anyway, I hope you find here something enjoyable or useful. Feel free to hit me up if you want collaborate or even just talk.
